I am Ph.D. Candidate in Economics at the University of California, Santa Barbara.

My research lies at the intersection of **public**, **urban**, and **labor economics**.

One strand of my work studies how corporate taxation affects firm behavior, with a focus on employment and investment decisions. A second strand examines the pass-through of value-added taxes into housing prices and how tax policy affects property characteristics and buyers' and sellers' behavior.
